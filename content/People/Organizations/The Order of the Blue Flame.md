An ancient order created to help control the rampant [[Blue Flames]] and help mitigate the effects of The [[Spell Plague]]. 

Though they have multiple bases their main base is [[Talathgard]] which was built into Mount Talath. 

They left notes with anyone important in every town and village, detailing what to do if the [[Spell Plague]] occurs again. 

They have been surprisingly absent since the start of the second spell plague.
