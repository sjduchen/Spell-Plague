Fflar fills the unassuming roll of [[content/Places/Waylain and Local Areas/Waylain]]'s bartender

In actuality his role is as the protector of [[0 Starleaf]] and [[The Tree of Souls]]. 

He works in tandem with [[0 Khelben]] and the #moonstars to ensure that [[content/People/Gods/Mystra]]'s rebirth goes as normal and she is not usurped by anyone.
