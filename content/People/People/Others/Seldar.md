A powerful High Elf chosen by the council of High Elves to replace [[content/People/Gods/Mystra]], both to restore what was lost during [[The Sundering]] and bring order to [[content/Places/The Weave]]. The elves believe her constant struggles leave its power at risk. 

His [[content/Artifacts/Divine Spark]] allows him to claim the power of Mystra without being destroyed. 

He wears a full suite of Adamantine armor, as does his horse. 

He advised the party that his plan to replace her is in motion and that should they help him, they will be rewarded. 

[[content/The Party/Honor]] was offered the chance to be purified and transformed into an elf.

Seldar was clear that beyond all else, that his responsibility is to the elven people. 

Height: 6'7"
