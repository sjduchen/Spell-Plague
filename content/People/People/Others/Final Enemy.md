
Karsus's prodigal apprentice. 

Seeking revenge for the death of her master Karsus's apprentice rebuilt her masters spell to finish his mission and revive him. 

Across 30 days she absorbs the divinity of Mystra, slowly turning herself into a god, and claiming control of the weave.
