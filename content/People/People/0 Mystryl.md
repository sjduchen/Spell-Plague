
#mystryl  is the original goddess of [[content/Places/The Weave]]. Three thousand years ago she allowed herself to die to [[content/People/People/Dead/Karsus]], knowing that her rebirth would repair the structure of the weave. 

Mystryl's [[content/Artifacts/Divine Spark]] was split into several pieces and spread throughout the weave. 

Her spark was split into fourpieces: 

1) [[Boundless]]: The spark of her limitless creative powers.
2) [[Foresight]]: The spark released from the chest (position 7) in [[content/Places/Waylain and Local Areas/Mystra's Hall of Echos/Mystra's Hall of Echos]] 
3) [[Chronicle]]: This spark is woven deep into the [[The Knotted Lands]], ancient magical powers having bound and buried it. 
4) [[content/People/Gods/Mystra]], the living portion of Mystryl, though unaware of what she is, Mystra knows that something inside of her is missing. 

[[The Chosen]] 

Mystryl had very few chosen, the only one left in play is an incredibly ancient and powerful Lich [[content/People/People/Dead/Larloch]].
