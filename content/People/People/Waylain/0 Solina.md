The spell scar mage. 

Description: Solina is a tall woman wrapped in black robes with a hood covering her head.

Intro description: A ghoul with a mouth at its stomach, chewing on a torn off limb. swings its arms at a figure clad in all black robes. As the ghouls hand flys through the air a trail of blue flames follows. It collides with a staff held by the figure.  With a good deal of force the figure shoves it back. You see the ghouls full body covered in glowing blue scars as though it’s on fire it’s twisted face barely resembles a human and the sharp teeth puncturing the flesh of a torn off arm are the only indicator that a stomach is there. 
At their feet are the distinct marks of a fight with a mage. Burns, acid melted stone and fragments of ice melting in warm blood can be found in the small circle the performed their deadly dance. In its center a small body wrapped in white robes lay at the center of the pool of blood. The white robes slowly soaking up the blood and the figures breathing beginning to slow. 


Solina is severely injured from her fight and has cast her spell slots for the day she can make do with cantrips in a pinch but would prefer to avoid participation in combat when able. 

Dark? In darkness a faint glow of blue seeps through the black robes from her right hand to the center of her back. The sides of her face illuminated beneath the hood as well. 


The first NPC the players see. If they assist their fight, Solina will join their party. If they don’t She will die taking the ghoul with her.
