An elven woman the party met inside of [[Talathgard]] after escaping from a horde of [[Spell Plagued Ghouls]] 

The party found her fighting for her life next to her dying brother [[content/People/People/Waylain/Somarin]].

She was gravely injured in the fight but was saved from death, alongside her brother, by [[content/The Party/Keth]] and [[content/The Party/Honor]] before being brought into [[content/Places/The Weave]] and left at an inn in [[content/Places/Waylain and Local Areas/Waylain]].

She was brought to Talathgard by a stronger wizard friend who turned into a Spell Plagued Ghoul. 

Description: Solina is a tall woman wrapped in black robes with a hood covering her head.
