An elven woman the party met inside of [[Talathgard]] after escaping from a horde of [[Spell Plagued Ghouls]] 

The party found her fighting for her life next to her dying brother [[Somarin]].

She was gravely injured in the fight but was saved from death, alongside her brother, by [[Keth]] and [[Honor]] before being brought into [[The Weave]] and left at an inn in [[Waylain]].

She was brought to Talathgard by a stronger wizard friend who turned into a Spell Plagued Ghoul. 

Description: Solina is a tall woman wrapped in black robes with a hood covering her head.
