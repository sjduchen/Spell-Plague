Blackstaff 

A [[Chosen]] of [[content/People/Gods/Mystra]]. He entered [[content/Places/The Weave]] aided by [[content/People/People/Waylain/Starleaf]] along side [[content/The Party/M]] who was brought in on accident. 

Khelben is from Waterdeep and was the same mage that brought [[content/The Party/Honor]] into [[content/Places/The Weave]], but does not know why [[The Order of the Blue Flame]] left a request to send anyone into The Weave. He fulfilled this request to repay a debt to them. 

He works alongside [[Fflar]] and Starleaf to protect Mystra from being overthrown, and is aided by his own group known as the [[content/People/Organizations/Moonstars]].
