Blackstaff 

A [[Chosen]] of [[Mystra]]. He entered [[The Weave]] aided by [[Starleaf]] along side [[M]] who was brought in on accident. 

Khelben is from Waterdeep and was the same mage that brought [[Honor]] into [[The Weave]], but does not know why [[The Order of the Blue Flame]] left a request to send anyone into The Weave. He fulfilled this request to repay a debt to them. 

He works alongside [[Fflar]] and Starleaf to protect Mystra from being overthrown, and is aided by his own group known as the [[Moonstars]].
