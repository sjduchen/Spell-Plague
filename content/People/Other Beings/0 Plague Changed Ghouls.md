
Adventurer's who were also teleported to Talathgard that were consumed by the blue flames of the weave. The flames drive them insane and transform them into the ghouls. 

Plague-changed Ghoul (5e Homebrew Variant) 

_Medium undead, chaotic evil_

- **Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 36 (5d8+10)
- **Speed:** 30 ft., climb 30 ft. 

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|15 (+2)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|7 (-2)|10 (+0)|8 (-1)|

- **Damage Resistances:** Force
- **Damage Immunities:** Poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, Exhaustion, Poison
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Common
- **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) 

**Plague Mutation.** The ghoul has a large, toothed maw on its stomach.  
**Spellscarred.** The ghoul has resistance to force damage. 

Actions

**Multiattack.** The ghoul makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws or gut maw.  
**Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. _Hit:_ 10 (2𝑑6+3) piercing damage.  
**Claws.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 7 (2𝑑4+2) slashing damage. Instead of paralyzing, the target is pushed 5 feet directly away from the ghoul.  

**Gut Maw.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature that is prone, restrained, or paralyzed. _Hit:_ 12 (2𝑑8+3) piercing damage, and the ghoul regains hit points equal to half the damage dealt.
