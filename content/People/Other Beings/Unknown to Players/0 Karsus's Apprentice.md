Vysaria Telunwe

An ancient sea-elf, thousands of years old. 

Raised by [[Karsus]] after her family rejected her for her incredible magical power. 

The Sea Elves, an already reclusive race wrote away all notices of her existence after she left, ashamed of her magical proficiency and how it connected her to the other elven races. 

She grew quickly under the tutelage of Karsus and had a deep understanding of his magical skills. 

She was present, hidden away under immense magical distortions during [[Karsus's Folly]]. When it was clear her master would die she was the one who split [[Mystryl]]'s divine spark, and she had fled with his notes. 

Motivation: She is determined to succeed where her master Karsus had failed. By becoming the new goddess of [[spellplague/content/Places/The Weave|The Weave]] she will prove her entire race wrong about the value of magic.
