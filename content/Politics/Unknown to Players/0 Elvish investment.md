The elves have a vested interest in replacing [[Mystra]] as the goddess of [[The Weave]]. While they do not know who is killing her they know this is their best chance to reconnect themselves to the weave as they had once been before 

[[Seldar]] having succeeded in [[Blade Rite]] proved himself worthy to lead elves, and is duty bound to restore [[Elven High Magic]] 

[[Corellon]] has been silent to those attempting to communicate with him regarding how the Elves should proceed. Many think of this as a bad decision. 

Elvish plans had been in place long before the start of this story, they had always expected another attempt and while they would never commit the act they’d also never miss the chance to take advantage of the situation
