Few gods assist players during the events of the game, any player attempts to commune with their gods even in dire circumstances is rare. 

Many High Priests of all religions die begging their gods for divine aid or guidance. While they are granted power from their gods they do not get much else. 

Most of the gods held back their interaction out of fear that if Mysta falls they would start a divine war. 


Long term this will also lead to the Second Time of Troubles where gods return to the mortal plane. 

[[content/People/Gods/Corellon]] a known ally of Mystra has stayed out of this rebirth and refuses to acknowledge his worshipers in this time.
