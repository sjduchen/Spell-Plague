General
-------------------
Waylain is a small town about an hours walk from a nearby mountain range. It is made up of 8 buildings forming a square, in its center are several small tables and a raised platform at about 10x10' 

The buildings were all made from mismatched wooden boards, nailed together and set into stone. Thatch roofs well maintained and the stained glass windows were incredibly clean. The town itself was lain out well and maintained, it appeared to suffer more from a lack of resource than effort.
