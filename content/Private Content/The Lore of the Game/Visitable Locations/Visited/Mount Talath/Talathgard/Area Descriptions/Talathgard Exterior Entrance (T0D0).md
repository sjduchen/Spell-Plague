
Two rows of stone pillars ten pillars deep mark the entrance way into the Talathgard fortress. Each pillar bears the scars of war,  long straight cuts from swords and axes, circular holes, and fragments of obsidian melded into the stone, but all the pillars had been weathered the same none stood out from the other, none had been rebuilt. 

Sunlight fills the walk way into the fortress, the pillars casting shadows against the walls. Two Golden doors, their frame flickering light blue as though they are ablaze mark the edge of the sunlight and the entrance. 

The ground is a pale tan colored packed dirt.
