
Goal
----------
Players must solve the room puzzle to cause a book to appear with more information

Description
------------------
As the party walks into the room on the left wall they see a desk and chair pulled a few tiles away from an empty bookshelf. Adjacent to the bookshelf is a chest, also empty. 

Against the right wall, centered with the room, is an empty bookshelf with only one shelf installed at the middle section. 

Past the a curtain divides the room in half if players proceed to the other half they will see a desk pulled a few tiles away from an bookshelf: 

It has Three shelves, the Top and bottom contain books of descending size moving inward the largest outer book on each shelf is blue, then yellow, the red, then green as they move inward.  The middle shelf is empty. 


False solution
---------------------
Players move the left or right half creating symmetry with the room, a book will appear telling a false tale of the order of the blue flame and how they had always existed as protectors of magical law

Real Solution
-------------------
Players move all items in both the chest and book shelves to the opposite side of the room. No order necessary, they need only have the items cross the curtain dividing the room. A book will appear telling of Karsus and the origin of the blue flames and how Mystryl died to be reborn as Mystra to save the weave. 

Secret Third Option
----------------------------
Players may instead walk to the bookshelf and attempt to spin the shelf in place, it will open a small pantry with 3 books inside. 
The false solution | The real solution | A book detailing how to reset the room
