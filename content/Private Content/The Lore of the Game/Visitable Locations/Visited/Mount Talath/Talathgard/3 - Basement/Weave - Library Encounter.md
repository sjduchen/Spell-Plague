The library, filled with ancient knowledge on the spell plague, is designed to fight the spell plague. If anyone enters the room effected by spell plague or a spell scar, multiple books throughout the room come alive and attack the party. They focus their attention on those effected by the plague first. 


Scaling difficulty - 1 book per player that has a spell scar and books appear as they disturb the library.
