

If players enter through the main lobby stair case  they will find a large room with 10 rows of 4 circles and a thick white line drawn from wall to wall 2 feet before the hallway the room leads into. 

The circles are just the First two letters of each race

AA  AA    AA   (Aasimar)
DR  DR   DR    (Dragonborne)
DW DW  DW   (Dwarf)
EL   EL    EL      (Elf)
GN  GN   GN    (Gnome)
GO  GO   GO   (Goliath)
HA  HA    HA   (Halfling)
HU  HU   HU    (Human)
OR  OR   OR    (Orc)
TI    TI     TI      (Tiefling)


The floor boards are incredibly creeky in this area. and get worse as players get closer to the front. 


If players cross the white lines step on any circles, or step on the floor outside the circles nothing happens and they are completely unaffected.
