Description
-----------------
* Piles of glowing blue flames lay spread across the room, though they do not spread. Atop boxes and tables they sit in place burning endlessly. The room has been torn apart, shattered glass, turned over tables and chairs and burn marks cover the books and desks, almost as though they burn something specific. 

Puzzle
------------
* Two parts 
	* **Turn on the anti magic field to stop the mephits** 
		* Hook/Hint: A note at the smaller desk:
					"Enmore, please prepare samples AD9 and AX5, if you run low on the priming liquid, or have difficulty with the specimens remember solutions are always in my cabinet" 
			* Solution: Find the lever inside the cabinet and activate the anti-magic field.
	* **Determine the phrase word for the room:**
		* Hook/Hint: (written above the door) Order is knowledge, knowledge is organized 
		* Hint two: As the party moves through the room the fragments of glass shine bright in the eyes of their eyes, far to bright to be torchlight. But there does not appear to be another source. (if players clean the glass away they see a letter formed on the ground from the shadow of a slightly uneven piece of flooring.)
			* Consequence: cleaning the whole room takes an hour, tracing the letters and scanning the floor takes two hours. 
			* Solution: Players clean the room and move the bookshelf back in place, while obscured before 
			* Phrase word revealed is: 

Traps
------------
* Shattered Glass: 
	* The floor is covered in broken glass!
	* No shoes? DC 10 DEX Save every 15 feet or take 1d2 DMG and DC 5 CON Save or yell.

Enemies
-------------
* Blue Flame Mephits (Level 1: 4 Mephits, Level 2: , Level 3: )
	* All Mephits start asleep.
	* Critical hits against players cause DC 15 CON save or gain a spell scar. 
	* Quiet noises may wake the closest, party has 1 round to kill before others are altered.
	* Will fall prone in place when the anti magic field is triggered. 

Area
-------------
* Research tables confirm Mephits are spellplagued, don't indicate lake of history. 
* Small boxes hold references to spell plagued ghouls, references are made frequently to those with Spell Scars
* Cabinets contain beakers, vials, and 1 of them has a lever that activates an anti-magic zone.
