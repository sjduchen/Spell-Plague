The original spell had a casting time of 6 hours to complete the theft of a gods power. His apprentice had fled with his notes after the failure and dedicated their life to revising and correcting where they went wrong. 

They learned that the spells duration had simply been to short and to far from the god. The only way to steal the power of a god was slowly, consuming it day by day so not to overwhelm the soul

The apprentice had rebuilt the spell to have a casting time of 30 days. Her murder of Mystra would be like a cancer spreading through her slowly replacing Mystra with a deep black magical ooze.  

The imperfections in the spell are the cause of the blue flames running rampant
