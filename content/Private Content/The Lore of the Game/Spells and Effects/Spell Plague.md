Spell Plague the representation of magic. [[Blue Flames]] going wild on all planes connected to [[The Weave]] anyone who uses magic in the wrong place or uses the maximum amount of magic possible at once risks being turned into one of the Spell Plagued. Some may get lucky and instead get a [[Spell Scar]] 

As [[Mystra]] dies the spell plague grows faster, consuming everything around it. 

Progression Stages
-----------------------------
1) At 12PM on the first day, Mystra had been struck with the blow that starts her demise. When Myshra begins to die the most powerful artifacts and spell casters are effected first. This drives the most powerful casters who are actively casting or using incredible amounts of magic completely insane. This power flows through them and into anyone they using magic on when it happens. 
2) Outside of the weave spells cast at the highest level player is able to, must make DC 9 (+1 per level of spell cast) luck check. On a fail they must pass a DC 10 saving throw against their casting modifier. 
3) Skill checks on arcana for those without proficiency are performed with disadvantage. The instability of the weave muddles the minds of inexperienced mages.
4) Once per day a cast spell may instead use life to cast instead of spell slots. If a player attempts to cast a spell at a level they have without spell slots available to use they take 1d4 damage per spell level (up to level 4: then its +2 per level with each die increasing (5 = 5d6, 6 = 6d8, 7 = 7d10, 8=8d12, 9=9d20) The damage is dealt first. each time a player casts a spell they roll a d100 against a dc 25 check (+5 per spells cast per day)This effect happens only once per day, and players may intentionally do it if they figure it out. 
5) Once per day (at 12) on random item per player gains sentience for 3 hours.Players Roll 1d8 to pick an emotion, that item communicates aloud and epitomizes that emotion. Angry,Sad,Bored,Hungry,Confused,Happy,Disgusted,Fearful.
6) Their lives perpetually connected to the weave now pose a risk to them.  Magical beasts begin to hibernate so they wont die ( this will be more of a world effect than one that bothers the players)
7) Any powerful item has a risk of becoming mundane on this day and again each subsequent day.The owner of the magic item makes a DC 15 Luck check at 12pm every day for each magic item or it becomes mundane on a fail.  Players with Attunned items roll with advantage. 
8) Although portals and other magical forms of transportation had failed, Planar Gates had stood for longer than expected. Almost as though the weave itself had fought to maintain them instead of destroying them. Could Mystra have been the one to do this?
