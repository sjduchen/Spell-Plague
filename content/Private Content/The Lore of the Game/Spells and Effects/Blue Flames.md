The physical manifestation of magic. These flames occur when [[content/People/Gods/Mystra]] loses control and they actively destroy anything using most of their magical potential. Blue flames are the cause of the [[content/Private Content/The Lore of the Game/Spells and Effects/Spell Plague]] and those harmed by them that do not fully turn into Spell Plagued, gain a [[content/Private Content/The Lore of the Game/Spells and Effects/Spell Scar]]. 

An ancient order known as [[The Order of the Blue Flame]], had spent years trying to control the spread and destruction of the blue flames as well as better understand how the Spell Plague came to be.
