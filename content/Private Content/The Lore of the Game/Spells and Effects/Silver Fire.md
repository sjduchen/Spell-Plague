The only known magical power capable of undoing a [[Dead Magic Zone]]. 

This power is granted to [[content/People/Gods/Mystra]]'s chosen and can be used to annihilate most enemies. It is the purest form of energy that can be pulled from [[content/Places/The Weave]]. 

Notably [[content/People/People/Waylain/Khelben]] is capable of using this power. There is also a [[Silver Flame Candle]] that can be located in [[content/Places/Waylain and Local Areas/Mystra's Hall of Echos/Mystra's Hall of Echos]]. 

If used correctly, one can use the power of Silver Fire to undo the effects of [[content/Private Content/The Lore of the Game/Spells and Effects/Spell Plague]] and even a [[content/Private Content/The Lore of the Game/Spells and Effects/Spell Scar]].
