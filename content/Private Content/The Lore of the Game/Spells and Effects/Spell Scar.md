Any player damaged by [[Blue Flames]] ue flames is at risk of gaining a spell scar. 

Luck Aspect
----------------------
Players roll a 1d4 when scarred: 
1) Their spell scar harms them for 1 point of health once per roll for 1 week and fades away. 
2) Their spell scar glows brightly, if not covered it is noticeable in the dark. Fades in 1 week
3) Their spell scar feels warm and powerful, once per day they can cast a weak fire spell, this does not fade
4) A stark blue line forms across their face, there is no other apparent benefit (this is the first of 3 effects needed to be able to overthrow Mysta)
