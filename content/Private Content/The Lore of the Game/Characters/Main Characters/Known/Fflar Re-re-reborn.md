Fflar fills the unassuming roll of [[Waylain]]'s bartender

In actuality his role is as the protector of [[Starleaf]] and [[The Tree of Souls]]. 

He works in tandem with [[Khelben]] and the #moonstars to ensure that [[Mystra]]'s rebirth goes as normal and she is not usurped by anyone.
