The Goddess who overseas [[The Weave]]. She was reborn in place of [[Mystryl]] 


[[The Chosen]] are those she has selected to serve her power, [[Khelben]] specifically is the only character present among them in this story, though he does have a group of like-minded people called [[Moonstars]] who aid him when necessary. 

Mystra's power is represented by [[Blue Flames]] that are the manifestation of magic. 

Mystra is only a partial rebirth of Mystryl and makes up the remainder of her [[Divine Spark]].
