A High Elf who successfully completed [[content/Spells and Magical Effects/Blade Rite]] to be recognized by the Elves as their rightful ruler. He is honor bound to fulfill one sole duty before taking his place as ruler of all Elves, restore the powers of High Magic that were lost to the Elven People after the [[First Sundering]].


His current plan is to overthrow [[content/Private Content/The Lore of the Game/Characters/Mystra]] who he believes to be a risk to all people. He knows of her prior deaths and believes that she is incapable of protecting and tending The Weave. He along with his party of loyal elves [[content/Private Content/The Lore of the Game/Characters/Main Characters/Known/Moliara/Moliara]], [[Adiamar]] have entered [[content/Places/The Weave]] to replace Mystra's [[content/Artifacts/Divine Spark]] with his own.

Seldar believes that the only way to restore high magic to the Elves, is by ascending to godhood. 

The process of moving his Divine Spark into Mystra's body requires the use of [[Elven High Magic]] that can be channeled inside The Weave with the use of an [[Anchor]]. His anchor is Adiamar.
