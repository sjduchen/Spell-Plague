The long journey to the Northern Town of Waylain was highly uneventful. Most of the journey had been through small groupings of trees and grasslands with worn down pathways leading towards the mountains, though given the emptiness of the skyline and terrain it would be difficult to get lost here. The air feels thick with tremendous magical energy but on your journey you had sensed no presence beyond your own. You have passed abandoned satchels of gold, healing potions, daggers, arrows, and rations along the way almost seemingly left behind but not even a cricket had made a noise on your 3 day journey. Notably though you had seen various saddles along the pathway, still strapped together with the sizing consistent. 

Your companions are in acceptable shape given their circumstances but are clearly ready to rest. 

As you continue your walk you begin to see buildings in the distance, and though you do not hear much noise from it, you can clearly see people moving about the town peacefully. 


As you near the town you can see 9 total buildings, almost as though aware of your presence two men step out of the building nearest you, clearly a tavern and begin to approach. 

To the left an large elven man wearing a laquered breastplate, a large kite shield on his back and a golden helm in his hand at his side. Attached to his hip is an ornate scabbard that despite his tall stature nearly reached the ground when he stepped on that side. His hair braided, and a russet color (reddish brown)

On the right a human man, whos height aligned with the elf's shoulder. He a large black cloak covered a green tunic. Though he appeared older his black hair and beared were missing the tell tale grey of age. In his right hand a large black staff he used almost as a walking stick, when held straight it reached above the height of the elven man by a few inches. at its tip a claw like shape.

Dylan you can feel the elven mans gaze fixated on you, the same gaze all orcs know from other races.
