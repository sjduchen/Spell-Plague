
A lich who was under the control of [[Larloch]], but had his phylactery corrupted by [[Blue Flames]]. 

The Lich has been driven completely mad and has stopped communicating with anyone. 

He was raising an army of undead had their phylactery consumed by spell plague driving it into madness. 

The Party must place the phylactery of the Lich into am enchanted goblet provided by Larloch. 

Doing so will give Larloch control over the Lich.
