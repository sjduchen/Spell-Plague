Players must navigate the cave, Larloch provided knowledge to the party that there are secret pathways inside the cavern that would serve as an emergency exit for an agent of the lich to escape with the phylactery. 


If players approach head-on or get caught, they will have to fight the roaming revenant and either win or evade it long enough to place the Phylactery inside Larlochs goblet. 

Doing so stabilizes the lich under Larlochs control and halts the revenant in its steps.
