

Teleportation
---------------------
* Regardless of your origins, all of you have made your way to Mount Talath by teleportation, you have been sent specifically due to your lack of magical experience, as fledgling adventurers you fit the bill of what The Order of the Blue Flame has preplanned. Each of you had been commanded by your local high mage to do the following:
  
  "You are to accept transport to Talath immediately. Once you arrive you are to make your way inside the fortress and proceed to its basement and step into the portal, you must step through within 7 days. When you step through, find and protect Mystra, no life is to be placed above hers, no life is more sacred. Whatever actions you take to ensure her life shall be accepted by the law. This is your duty, you are not alone in this duty, and your inexperience is why you must go." 
  
  The party steps out of their portal at 11:50AM, the sun at its peak. In front of them are 20 pillars of stone that appear not to have been built, but carved out of the cave. Each of them 20 feet apart, leading to a set of massive doors. Their golden color seems to flicker with faint hints of blue at this distance. Many people are in the area, some sweeping the ground to keep the packed dirt as clean as possible and others tending to horses, or talking to one another. 
  
  Behind them are the sounds of a nearby town, birds cawing, people talking. Players can see a town in the distance about a 20-30 minute walk. A large tavern built into the cliff face. 
  
  There is a sharp scent in the air, almost like burning acid, similar to the smell of a portal. 
  
  As you get your bearings, you can see a tall man approach you (warlock leader / Fen Mon) further back towards the pillars you see a group of mages a muscular woman with a very large sword and a halfling with an axe twice his size.
  Fen Mon Approaches:
  
    (Players will be able to have a short interaction before Noon)
    
  "More of us I see, what have they told you all about why we are here?"
  
  * Fen mon should comment on their lack of equipment, as mention how he thinks the note was foolish. 
    
    

Noon
---------------

Screams from the town are the first thing the party hears, any who turn see the town ablaze, burning in bright blue flames. 
Then in a panic, portals begin appearing all around them. At first just a few, then 10s, then hundreds.  As people begin walking through, they catch fire. Their bodies burning with the same blue flames as the town. Their screams grow louder by the second. 

The first to arrive collapse, screams growing quieter and then silent. 

The nearest is a young man who appeared to be barely a man
	* Players who approach are at risk of attack and damage. Flames shoot from the boy 
	* Make a DC10 DEX SAVE or take 1DHIT DIE of damage - if you roll above half your die value make a DC 15 Con save or gain a spell scar. 


The first dead begin to rise again as plague changed ghouls tho they are not rotting, their skin sags and their burns glowing blue now, if players look closely they will see mouths formed and teeth at their stomachs pulling in their clothing. 

More mages continue to appear most catch flame but a few arrive safely able to react. The remaining mages immediately attempt to fight. As they cast their most powerful spells their spells rip them apart from the inside out. 

In the distance the sound of an explosion queues the start of combat
Combat starts
--------------------
Roll initiative, players below 10 will go after the shockwave hits 

DC 10 STR Check or take 1d4 bludgeoning and be knocked prone. 


Space the map out based on player speeds so they arrive within 2-3 rounds of the flames reaching them. 

Fen Mon will focus on helping party over others as they appear to be the least equipped.  
As they get close multiple ghouls will be attacking the door, Fen Mon will tell the players to find the gate into the weave and burst into flames casting fireball to clear the pathway. 

The door is cracked open enough to slip inside and close the door behind. 
 

as the party enters the Temple they witness 2 apprentice mages struggling to take down a lone plague ghoul, one of them is grievously wounded and fighting from the ground. He is in the middle of the flame pillars and clearly wounded but has no signs of harm from the plague.

3 choices: 

1) Help the mages defeat the ghoul
2) Run past them towards the gate deeper into the fortress
3) Take the stairway left towards the black doorway with a deep blue flame painted on it.
