
The party is drawn together to find a way into the weave. 

In some way each player has been effected by the re-emergence of the  #SpellPlague.

Anyone with a magical background see's the immediate effect of spell plague on those around them (protecting their important background characters)

Anyone with a non-magical focused background witnesses the second storm of blue fire first hand, watching as a neighboring town or city is fully engulfed. 

As young adventurers they are not as powerfully connected by the weave as their mentors and friends and those around them and are deemed the only hope to stop the plague.
