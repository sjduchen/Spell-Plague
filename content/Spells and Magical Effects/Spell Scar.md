A permanent mark on the body. Being harmed by the [[content/Spells and Magical Effects/Blue Flames]] formed from the collapse of [[content/Places/The Weave]] has a chance to inflict one. 

Very little is known to The Party about the effects of these scars. 

[[content/The Party/Aelar]] has received one across his face after attempting connect [[content/Spells and Magical Effects/Silver Fire]] and [[content/Spells and Magical Effects/Blue Flames]] inside [[content/Places/Waylain and Local Areas/Mystra's Hall of Echos/Mystra's Hall of Echos]].
