A permanent mark on the body. Being harmed by the [[Blue Flames]] formed from the collapse of [[The Weave]] has a chance to inflict one. 

Very little is known to The Party about the effects of these scars. 

[[Aelar]] has received one across his face after attempting connect [[Silver Fire]] and [[Blue Flames]] inside [[Mystra's Hall of Echos]].
