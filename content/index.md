**Session 2:** 

[[content/The Party/Aelar]] and [[content/The Party/M]] join party.

- After a journey north, finding loose gear, abandoned campsites, and odd saddles missing their beasts, they arrive at [[content/Places/Waylain and Local Areas/Waylain]].

* They are greeted by [[Fflar]] and [[content/People/People/Waylain/Khelben]], and learn that [[content/People/Gods/Mystra]]'s death, though rare, is cyclical and maintains [[content/Places/The Weave]]. Fflar Introduces the party to [[content/People/People/Waylain/Starleaf]] an ancient elf, though young in appearance. She explains that something is different this time and [[The Order of the Blue Flame]] is no where to be found. 
* Starleaf tells the party that they will learn more at [[content/Places/Waylain and Local Areas/Mystra's Hall of Echos/Mystra's Hall of Echos]], and introduces [[content/The Party/M]] to the party. They also accidentally free [[content/The Party/Aelar]] who was frozen in time.
* The party visits Mysta's Temple and learns of what happened to [[content/People/Gods/Mystryl]] and how Mystra came to be. They witness the power of [[content/Spells and Magical Effects/Silver Fire]] and how it appears to bring magic back into places without it and accidentally discovered a secret room where a magical recording left by Mystral reveals that Mystra is only part of her being and that 3 [[content/Artifacts/Divine Spark]]s split away preventing her true rebirth. 
* On their way out, they relive the horrors of their pasts through an illusion in an enchanted hallway
