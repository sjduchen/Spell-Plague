[[Mystryl]]'s [[Divine Spark]] representing her chaotic creativity. This spark was the same one the brought the party into [[The Weave]] from [[Talathgard]]. 

As it completed the journey into The Weave, it left behind an adaptive dagger known as [[The Weaver]].

Boundless takes the form of a different dagger hidden on [[Honor]]'s person alongside his other knives.

Must be attuned
+1 Weapon, as a free action its wielder can will the weapon to change shape into any simple or martial weapon that does not use a projectile. 

If the wielder has True Attunement: 
+3 Weapon, as a free action the wielder can will the weapon into any Non-magical weapon with any material type. Additionally it can fire basic projectiles without ammunition. 
Once per day you may cast any 1 spell of each level up to 4th. (No on the fly spell research)
