A Divine Spark is a spiritual essence that can be manifested into a physical object. Outside of incredible influence a divine spark is the representation of its owner and cannot be tainted or truly destroyed. Most known sparks belong to existing gods already. 

Some can form a divine spark inside themselves essentially making their souls immortal even if they can die.

The portfolio of a God is the source of its power, a Divine Spark can manifest that power into a God. Anyone with a Divine Spark and a means to access the spark of another god can replace it with their own, usurping them. In the case of this game this is the only means of [[Ascension]].
