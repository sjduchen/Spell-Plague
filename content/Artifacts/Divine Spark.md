A Divine Spark is a spiritual essence that can be manifested into a physical object. Outside of incredible influence a divine spark is the representation of its owner and cannot be tainted or truly destroyed. Most known sparks belong to existing gods already. 

There are few known divine sparks:

1) [[content/People/Gods/Mystryl]]'s spark was split into four when [[content/People/People/Dead/Karsus]] attempted to kill her. 
	1) [[content/People/Gods/Mystra]] reborn in her place.
	2) Divine Spark - Unknown Location
	3) Divine Spark - Unkown Location
	4) Divine Spark - Found by [[content/The Party/Honor]] in a chest in [[content/Places/Waylain and Local Areas/Mystra's Hall of Echos/Mystra's Hall of Echos]].

2) Divine Spark - This one brought the party into [[content/Places/The Weave]] from [[Talathgard]].

3) [[content/People/People/Others/Seldar]]'s spark, shown to the party on their arrival into The Weave.
