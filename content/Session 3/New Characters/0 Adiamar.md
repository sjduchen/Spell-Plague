#anchor 
A shorter woman by elven standards she is a powerful mage with even greater potential. 

Her duty is to serve as an [[Anchor]] for [[Seldar]]. 

She had to split off from Seldar's Party to learn more of the threats near [[Waylain]] and found herself captured while investigating the cavern. 

While captured she was treated well, but heavily questioned, mostly about Karsus and why she is here.

She will admit to the party that while she tried hard she was unable to hide why Seldar is here. 


Name: Adiamar

Motivation: Follow Seldar's lead and restore High magic to the elves.

Unique Characteristics: Powerful mage with great potential 

Skills: Able to function as an Anchor

Relevance: One of the most critical members of [[Seldar]]'s team. 

Connections: [[Seldar]], [[Moliara]]
