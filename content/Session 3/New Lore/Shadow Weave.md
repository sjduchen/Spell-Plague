An imitation of [[The Weave]], it channels much of the darker magical powers that some would wish untended by [[Mystra]]. 

Evil deities hold great control over this place, though notably [[0 Araushnee]] controls most of this place. 

Power here is less subdued, with spell slingers able to feel much more power in their combative magics. 

Stray beasts also roam these places unchecked, drawn to the power of it all.
