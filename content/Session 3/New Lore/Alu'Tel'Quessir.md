(Sea Elves)

Players will learn of them inside the [[Cavern of the Spell Plagued Lich.png]] inside one of the side rooms. 

Known to be among the least magically inclined elves, often rejecting those who are magical. They deem magic as a tool that is misused and only enables its wielders to wave away problems without truly understanding them. Those who speak of their magic depend on it.
