11) The smoke room. A small and incredibly tight entrance, there is a faint light coming out of it. If a player attempts to squeeze through the hole they will first feel a rush of wind move past them that will stop when they cover the hole.  Then must make a DC 15 dexterity check or become stuck in the hole if they are a medium sized creature. Larger they cannot attempt and smaller they will fit. If stuck the room will begin to quickly fill with smoke. Players will each get one action per round. At the end of round 1 the room will be filled with the smoke and the player will begin to inhale smoke (unless they are holding their breath). They can hold their breath for minutes equal to 1 + Con mod. DC 20 Strength Check to free, or a combined roll of 25 or greater for the entire party (stuck player has disadvantage) If out of breath the player loses 1d8 life per round until dead or free. 
12) Delayed Fireball Room. A thin trip wire at the floor connected to a delayed fire ball scroll that starts after lit. If tripped immediately a bottle of oil will shatter on the ground falling from the ceiling. (Passive perception 10 or above will notice movement out of the corner of their eye) Players will each have a chance to perform 2 actions and 1 movement before the spell triggers.
