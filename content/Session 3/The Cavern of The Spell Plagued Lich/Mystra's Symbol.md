
A circle of seven stars floating in red mist. 

Each star represents an aspect of Mystryl 

1) Ambition (Netherese)
2) Arrogance (Phaerimm)
3) Tradition (Elves)
4) Love of Magic (Chosen)
5) Humility (Karsus/His Apprentice)
6) Responsibility (Blue Flames/Spell Scared/ Spell Plagued/ Order member)
7) Truth (Mystryl's Divine Sparks)

The Mist represents the raw chaotic power of The Weave that must be controlled. (The red mist leaking from Mystra as she is dying)
