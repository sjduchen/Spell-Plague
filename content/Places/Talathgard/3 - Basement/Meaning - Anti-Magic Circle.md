Anti-magic zone and the control ceiling and the goblin drones  - zone keeps goblins alive and revives them and keeps them under control. Blood, sparks, magic and loud noises trigger the circle activating the null zone stopping all magic. Anyone with an unwrapped spell scar takes 1d2 damage per round in bleeding damage. 

Description 
----------------------
At the entrance to the room above the door the words "Might beyond Magic" are carved into the stone
* In the center of the room there are 4 Goblin warriors (level 2: 6) 
* Each goblin stands in place, weapon in hand but not moving or reacting to the players.
* If a player initiates a combat action or attempts to interact with the goblins in anyway they will here the sound of a tile sliding near the back door and the anti-magic circle will trigger.
* each goblin has a small stone embedded into their skull, it has faint traces of magic. 

Trap
--------------
In front of each goblin is a delayed pressure plate tile, if a player enters the room and stops on that tile it will trigger the rooms effect.

Combat 
---------------------
Goblins will focus on the nearest player, they only have bludgeoning weapons except for one in the back of the pack with a sword.

Goblins will focus on players who are standing before attempting to finish anyone who is downed. 

The anti-magic field will deactivate if players are knocked unconscious. 


Puzzle
--------------
kill the goblins and have the anti-magic field on, their blood spells out the the word meaning: 

Fight and kill the goblins without using magic their blood is pulled into the center of the room filling the word "meaning" with blood.
