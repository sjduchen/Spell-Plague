
A spherical room lined with thousands of precious gems. A glittering rainbow of colors made from the reflected light pouring in from the hallway.  The only gap in this is for a narrow stairway that leads down to a circular platform floating in the middle of the sphere. when the party makes it down and onto the platform the stairs disappear and the room is sealed leaving them in darkness for a brief moment.  A small stone rotates in place revealing a bright white gem. It lays there giving off a minimal amount of light.

Nothing happens until players pickup the sphere. 

When anyone touches the sphere the entire party will here this in their minds:

"You hold the divine spark of Mystryl, though she has been reborn a new her spark could not be lost and it was not taken. You are bound by the oaths of your families, bloodlines, and tribes to aid the order of the blue flame. The time has come to restore the rightful master of the weave. Enter the weave, find what has exposed Mystra's Spark and replace it. That is your sacred duty"

The pure white light grows, blindingly bright white light changes instantly thousands of colors emit outward each projected to the corresponding gem on the wall, the lights go through the party as though they were never there.  The gem and the room begin to spin faster and faster, then the rows begin to spin in alternating directions and then columns moving as well. The lights moving so quickly they leave thin traces behind like string, each disappearing then reappearing each pass faster than before, each string behind longer then before until all at once the party finds itself in the weave.
