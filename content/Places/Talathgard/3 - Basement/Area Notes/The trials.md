The dungeon section of Talathgard is designed to teach lessons to new recruits before they are allowed closer to the library of Mystryl 

Respect - players clear the armor room either by showing respect to those before them or winning the fights and proving they are worthy of respect 

Meaning - players must prove they have meaning in this world beyond magic by surviving the anti magic room 

Knowledge - players must prove they can use their knowledge to solve problems on their own by determining how to defeat the blue mephits. 

Each trial will provide players with a code word of the order of blue flame.  All 3 words will unlock the next area. 

The left room will open to those who are spell scarred and those who are willing to enter. Blue flames inside make it clear that entering will result in a spell scar for those uncorrupted. 

The right room has a magical barrier preventing blue flames and spell plagued from entering 

Players in the left will be told they are scarred and tainted by the flame but they can rise above its corruption and bring meaning to the world. Though they shall never be pure again they have value to the world. Their separation is not because of who they are but what they have become. They are plagued and their blood line will always be tainted by the flame. The spell scars could do nothing or they could set the world ablaze. they are told that one day may come where for the good of the world they may need to end their own lives 

Players on the right are told what those on the left will hear and warned that they have a solemn duty as the lure to kill them should the time come. That they will face the same risks as their friends if they continue with them and risk being horribly maimed. They see the scope of horror and potential power that a spell scar can have to express to them the gravity of the situation.

It tells that the flames are the least worrisome problem if the weave is to be claimed. Anyone with a divine spark could reshape the weave however they see fit and that power allows them to reshape and control what the blue flames can do. The flames are unguided power. But with direction they could be far far worse.
