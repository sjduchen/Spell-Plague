The Monodrones appear in response to the Ghouls getting within two layers of the library. 15 of them will enter the room with long book carts and begin to fill them with all the books in the library before proceeding down the long dark pathway they appeared from. It will take a total of 30 minutes for them to empty the library completely the first 15 are clearing the top height layer (1 quarter) , after which they empty a quarter of the library every 5 minutes. each group of 30 drones is followed and replaced by another group of 15. After which 5 total appear to collect any books loose on the ground or stored in secret places. 

The modrones will not interact with players and will move around them to collect books, while they will not take books from them they will collect any that players are not in physical contact with, including books in front of them. 

A player attempting to take a book will trigger a warning from all Modrones simultaneously. Proceeding past the warning with initiate combat with them, the drones are unarmed. 

A spell scared player attempting to take a book will cause combat to inititate immediately. 

If combat is initiated all Modrones in possession of a book will expend their full movement and action on their first turn to return the books they are carrying to a shelf. 

After which the drones will prioritize targets in the following order: 

Player who initiated combat by attempting to take a book -> spell scared -> players organized in terms of available spell slots. 

15 Modrones,  with reinforcements that appear every 5 minutes (first set after 10). The next set does not engage if combat is completed prior to their arrival.
