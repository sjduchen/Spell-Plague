
Through the modrone hallway in the library. An hours walk forward is a a small room with a planar portal inside. 

Description
--------------------
"The portal is made of stone alternating between blocks of  dark obsidian and white granite, thousands of runes are engraved and inlaid with the color of the opposite stones material"

Options
------------
* The portal is open and active if the party shoved through the monodrones or fought them. 
* It is closed if they did not. 
* Regardless to the right is a lever. When raised the portal opens to mechanus. When lowered the portal is closed. 

Pulling the lever and/or peering in
----------------------------
* If players look in to the portal they see the plane of Mechanus, and thousands upon thousands of Monodrones organizing equipment and siege weapons. 
* Players will hear a voice in their head "Do not interfere in the machinations of The One" 
  An ethereal like figure appears before them
  "Stand aside" (Dominate Creature) DC 30 Wisdom saving throw.
  The ethereal figure will shut the lever and snap it. The level of the cast and length of the cast will depend on how far the modrones progressed. Regardless players will be forced to stand in place watching Monodrones bring books from the library into Mechanus.
