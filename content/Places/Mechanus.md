The plane of Order. 

The primary life on this planet are the Modrones. 

They appear to be staging an invasion somewhere from what [[Keth]] and [[Honor]] witnessed while inside of [[Talathgard]]. They saw them taking books from a library inside and brining them through a planar portal to Mechanus. 

When looking inside they were stopped by a Secundi, an incredibly powerful mage who warned them not to interfere with [[The One]].
